# Framecase film quality

The owner rejected the first public episode on 2026-09-24. Its technical scene
reviews had passed, but three scenes were diagram-like inserts and the gallery
scene used a different rendering style. The next episode also changed the
protagonist's age and appearance between shots. Resolution alone was not the
problem.

New story direction requires a coherent hand-painted 2D style and the complete
character identity in each relevant shot prompt. At least half the shots,
including the opening, must feature an acting story character. The old emergency
clock, timeline and bell inserts cannot be silently used in a new publication.
Their original assets, failed generations, reviews and costs remain preserved.

After the existing transcript, prosody, exact-cut scene and timing gates, the
new creative review examines three exact final-master frames from every scene.
Overlapping groups retain neighboring cuts in longer films. It checks art
direction, character consistency, performance, composition, visual progression
and presentation-like filler. Positive scene scores do not override a creative
failure. The same master/package/window identity reuses its stored verdict,
including a negative verdict; it cannot buy repeated opinions until one passes.
Frame samples do not establish smooth motion or audio quality, so those checks
remain separate.

The gate was tested on the original published master, SHA-256
`f0f946b593b03bc964b407166d6a780956c16b3ed594bfb9f828eb13d2345962`.
It rejected the style clash, repeated framed footage and infographic staging.
The original public output and checkpoint were not changed by this review.

A private character-motion trial uses a frozen reference image and prompt,
the existing native commissioning journal, and one eight-second 1080p request
with sound. Its maximum listed video cost is $0.64. It has no publishing
permission, no automatic safety rewrite and no provider fallback. A missing
submission acknowledgement cannot trigger another POST. Trial media are
development assets, not proof that a complete episode passed all publication
checks. New frames were created with the built-in image generation tool and
kept as separate versioned assets.

An owner publication hold or disabled Framecase plan prevents both new and
already queued continuations, including after a deployment changes the build.
The channel's paused plan and publication hold are editorial state; global
Celery consumers and other channels continue operating.

On 2026-09-24 the owner watched revision `27507bbc-1798-58f5-96bc-5028aff6b4bf`
and approved its visual direction. Its Mira reference is now a hash-bound
repository asset. New episodes create a retained series cast sheet and 2K
keyframes through Fal Nano Banana 2 Edit, then animate those exact images through
Veo 3.1 Lite I2V at 1080p. A still-image review precedes motion generation; the
final film review also compares Mira to the approved series reference.

Keyframes use the separate persistent native journal prefix
`youtube_studio:commissioning:v1:framecase_image:`. Their existing owner authority
and connection must pass; each original root allows at most six image requests
for Shorts or 32 for a long, independently of the unchanged video cap. Requests
are reserved before POST and raw provider responses remain encrypted. Accepted
requests resume by GET; ambiguous submission, refusal, or changed billing cannot
trigger an alternate model or fresh POST. Every later release audit must include
these image journals alongside the video and archived voice-credit journals.

Reviewed list ceilings are $0.12 per 2K image and $0.08 per generated video second
(1080p with audio). Fal's authenticated base-price endpoint is checked hourly,
using the documented 1.5 image-resolution and 1.6 video-resolution multipliers.
A price increase blocks a new request rather than increasing its allowance.
This is setup commissioning, not a claimed final monthly cash cap or invoice.
The original commissioning authority stops admitting requests when the owner
enables the regular cash budget. No new subscriptions or topups are made.

Native scene sound is trimmed to the exact frame windows, normalized quietly,
and ducked below the unchanged narration. Picture packets are copied unchanged.
The mixed master receives a separate transcript check before publication, so
unrequested generated speech cannot inherit the original narration's approval.

The one-time `framecase_editorial_revision.activate` operation replaces the held
second episode's future plan entry and preserves the old plan, source hold,
checkpoint hash, native request hash and every original record. The new entry
retains the same canonical episode and exactly accepted narration boundaries.
The ordinary scheduler reserves the new root and daily slot; no operator
completion or fake public receipt is inserted. This operation is never called by
automatic recovery and cannot repeatedly mint revision budgets.
