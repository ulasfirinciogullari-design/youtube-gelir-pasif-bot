# Full documentary narration

Short Kie Gemini tests did not establish full documentary reliability. All
three actual takes for each of the two queued documentaries remain recorded,
including their original audio, costs and blind recognition. Several takes
stopped before the last scenes. No old receipt, reservation or attempt is reset.

Fal ElevenLabs Turbo v2.5 is evaluated separately using the exact original
scripts. The two full trials each have one durable request slot. Accepted
queue jobs are only polled; unknown submissions cannot be repeated. The
original and complete normalized MP3s remain in object storage. Native returned
character alignment establishes scene boundaries, never a speech approval.

Turkish qualification passed real independent Scribe recognition and Gemini
prosody review for the full 167.64-second normalized performance. The activation
operator recomputes both judgments from their immutable provider responses.
English qualification has not passed and remains disabled in this release.
Two initial Turkish recognizer reservations with missing outcomes remain held;
the normal production loudness derivative has separate captured observations.

One same-root continuation reuses the already-qualified Turkish performance
without another TTS request. Future authorized Capital documentaries can use
the qualified route, with three fixed attempt slots on the original root.
An unknown submit blocks subsequent attempts. Native and Kie historical roots
retain their assignments, except the explicitly qualified trial continuation.
The complete prepared bytes are reused through the voice stage, retaining the
existing speech evidence. Final mixed-audio, source, editorial, visual and
publication checks remain mandatory.

Commissioning authority is separate from the owner's final operating budget.
This route requires the existing commissioning foundation and stops admitting
new paid work when it is replaced by an operating budget. The conservative
list estimate is $0.05 per 1,000 characters rounded upward to whole thousands,
at most $0.25 per request. These are estimates, not invoices or a newly chosen
monthly budget. No balance allocation, subscription or top-up is created.
