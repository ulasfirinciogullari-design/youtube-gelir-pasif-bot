# Fal video selection reviewed 2026-09-23/24

The owner requested economical alternatives to Veo. These are published list-price
comparisons, not invoices or measured quality equivalence. Narration, research,
review, editing, rendering, storage and failed requests are additional costs.
Only the already integrated, priced endpoints are selectable in production.

| Model / official source | Listed silent-video cost | Production decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Seedance 1.5 Pro](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/bytedance/seedance/v1.5/pro/text-to-video) | About $0.13 for 5 seconds at 720p; token-based estimate rounded up | Prospective automatic choice; full independent QA required |
| [Seedance 1 Pro Fast](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/bytedance/seedance/v1/pro/fast/text-to-video) | About $0.11 for 5 seconds at 720p | Integrated explicit alternative; lower price alone does not establish equal quality |
| [Veo 3.1 Lite](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/veo3.1/lite) | $0.03/second at silent 720p; 5-second requirement buys 6 seconds, $0.18 | Integrated explicit choice; existing accepted requests remain here |
| [Veo 3.1 Fast](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/veo3.1/fast) | $0.10/second at silent 720p/1080p | More expensive; not the general default |
| [Wan 2.2 Turbo](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/wan/v2.2-a14b/text-to-video/turbo) | $0.10 per 720p video | Promising trial candidate; duration contract and actual scene QA need verification before integration |
| [Wan 2.5 Preview](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/wan-25-preview/text-to-video) | $0.10/second at 720p | Higher cost than current candidates |
| [Kling 3 Standard](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/kling-video/v3/standard/text-to-video) | $0.084/second without generated audio | Consider only if a measured scene-quality benefit justifies the cost |
| [PixVerse V6](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/pixverse/v6/text-to-video) | $0.045/second silent 720p | Additional candidate; not yet integrated or quality-tested here |
| [MiniMax Hailuo 02 Standard](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/minimax/hailuo-02/standard/text-to-video) | $0.045/second at 768p; 6 seconds $0.27 | Higher estimated cost for the current scene length |
| [LTX 2.3 Fast](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/ltx-2.3/text-to-video/fast) | Page sections showed conflicting $0.04/$0.06 per second at 1080p | Resolve price contract before paid routing |

Seedance 1.5 Pro and Fast estimates use 1280×720, 24 fps, five seconds and
the published video-token formula. Model-native generation duration can exceed
the final edit length. Silent clips are used because separate narration is
already produced and reviewed. Safety checks remain on.

This review covers representative cost/quality candidates, not every Fal endpoint
and not a paid benchmark of every model. No second paid model is submitted when
the first request's outcome is unknown or has hit a content filter. Accepted
requests retain their original model, request identity and charge history.

The existing two missing Margin assets were recovered by reading already accepted
Veo Lite results, with no new generation or speech request. That recovery is
independent of the prospective default for new productions.
